📜 Rules & Regulations

1. Responsible SOS Usage

Hold the app SOS button for 3 seconds (or press the spring-loaded red button on the Suraksha Kalam marker) only during genuine emergencies. False alerts to 112/1091 are punishable under Indian law.

2. Trusted Contact Verification

Keep at least 2 active family or guardian numbers saved in your Trusted Emergency Contacts list so offline GSM SMS alerts always reach someone nearby.

3. Hardware Care & Battery Safety

Keep the Suraksha Kalam marker dry, charge its battery regularly before travel, and ensure an active SIM with SMS balance is inserted inside the SIM800C slot.

4. Privacy & Location Consent

Your live GPS coordinates and audio recordings remain encrypted on your device and are shared strictly with chosen contacts and emergency services when SOS triggers.
